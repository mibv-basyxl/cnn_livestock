Operational Process

Field Officer Standard Workflow

A streamlined mobile-friendly process guiding field officers and veterinary technicians from the farmstead to central Ministry decision support.

1

Authenticate

Secure sign-in with role-scoped LGA credentials on mobile, tablet or workstation.

2

Register Farmer

Capture national identity, contact, LGA, ward, and community in under 2 minutes.

3

Tag & Profile Animal

Affix official ear tag, select species/breed, sex, age, and initial health status.

4

Administer Health Log

Record vaccinations, batch numbers, clinical diagnoses, and next booster dates.

5

Sync & Surveillance

Submit immediate outbreak radar alerts or routine sync directly to Calabar HQ.
